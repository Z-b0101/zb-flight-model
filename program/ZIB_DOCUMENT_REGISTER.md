## How to use this register

- **Update on every document change:** version bump, status change, or distribution change → new row entry or in-place update with the date, same day.
- **Quarterly review:** the GM puts the register on the integration review agenda; documents with no consuming decision within one gate cycle are challenged under the anti-theater rule.
- **Status definitions:** living (actively maintained) · proposed (needs approval to take effect) · ratified (accepted into the program record) · draft (not yet accepted) · superseded (replaced; kept for history).
- **Distribution definitions:** CEO-only (CEO + GM) · leadership (CEO, GM, Chief Engineer, future directors) · team (all hires) · external (shared outside the company — requires explicit CEO approval).
- This register is the authority on what exists. If a document isn't listed here, it isn't controlled.
