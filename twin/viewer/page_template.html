Z!B PoC Twin

The 0.90 m MH60 flying wing, built entirely from the controlled parameter file: planform and camber feed a vortex-lattice model, which feeds a generated JSBSim aircraft that flies the mission to battery depletion. Every input carries its status, so what is measured, what is derived and what is still assumed stays visible.

Climb at 45% throttle to +60 m, then hands-off cruise at the best-range trim until the usable energy is spent. The open-loop climb zooms about 25 m past the level-off point before settling, so peak altitude reads near 205 m.
